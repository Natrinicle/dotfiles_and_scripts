# Prompt templates

Step A generates the closed page. Step B edits that image id.

## A — closed subject

Black-and-white coloring page, pure white background, bold even black outlines, no gray, no color. One complete [SUBJECT], front view, no surprise interior, no fold lines. Large colorable shapes.

## B — split and fill (edit the A image)

Take this exact coloring page. Split the drawing down the vertical center. Slide the left half to the left third and the right half to the right third. Do not redraw those halves. Reconstruct the missing mid-body in the gap, then add [REVEAL] on that body. Reveal lines meet the cut-edge mouth or chest. Draw perfectly straight vertical dashed fold lines, 90 degrees to the page edge. White background, no color, no gray.

## One-detail fix

Keep this coloring page identical except [DETAIL]. Do not restyle faces, folds, feet, spots, or outer segments.

## Robot A

Complete sealed toy robot, visor down, chest panel shut, both arms and boots visible.

## Robot B

Split this exact robot down the middle of the head and chest. Move the halves apart. Fill the gap with circuit boards, ribbon cables, springs, and a circular core that grow from both cut chest edges. Keep the original halves. Dashed folds on the cuts.

## Frog B

One unforked frog tongue per mouth. Smooth sticky ribbon attached at the front of the mouth, paddle tip, fly on the tip. Not forked. Not spiral.
