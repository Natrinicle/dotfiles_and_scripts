# Fold geometries

## gate-fold-thirds (default)

Landscape US letter. Folds at 1/3 and 2/3 width.

```
UNFOLDED (one figure):  [ L structure ][ center = structure + reveal ][ R structure ]

FOLDED preview:         [ L structure | R structure ]
                        same pixels, center strip dropped
```

The character is one drawing that crosses both folds. The center strip is extra interior, not a second object.

- Apple: shared outline crosses both folds; center slice is the wormy core
- Dragon: body crosses both folds; center slice is open mouth and flame
- Robot: head rim, shoulders, chest-plate edges, hips cross both folds; center slice is the open chassis

If the three panels do not form one silhouette, the master failed.

Fold dashes are ruler-straight, 90 degrees to the sheet edge. Not drawn along anatomy.

## horizontal-center

Portrait letter. One fold at mid height. Folded preview is a crop of the outer (usually top) half. Do not redraw.

## Script detection

1. Pair of vertical dashed lines near 1/3 and 2/3
2. Else equal thirds
