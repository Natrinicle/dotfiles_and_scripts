## How to use a row

1. Open the URL.
2. Pick two examples in the **same** product category as the brief (bank vs
   toy vs docs).
3. Note: type pairing, density, motion trigger (hover vs load), footer
   structure, icon optical size.
4. Put those notes in the four-line brief. Do not paste their CSS.
