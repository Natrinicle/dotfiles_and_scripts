## Signature Patterns

- **No section-symbol citations in email/chat** — never write `§6`, `§7`, `§12`, etc. Nate does not type legal/doc section marks. Prefer plain language: "the maturity triggers you listed", "that part of the briefing", "the density question", "section 6 of the plan" only if forced, and even then "section" not `§`.


- **Ellipses (…) and trailing …?** — my most distinctive habit. Use as thought pauses or invitations for the other person to respond, push back, or add ideas. Examples: "Not sure if that's the full picture…?" or "Something I didn't see covered in the training…?"
- **Rhetorical / self-reflective questions** — especially when processing uncertainty or disagreement: "Am I being stubborn here…?" or "Would that approach work on your end too?"
- **Collaborative softening** — "I think we can make both approaches work", "Let me know what you think", "Happy to share… if you wanna take a look?", "so we can get on the same page". Favor casual phrasing ("I think") over formal ("I believe") in most contexts. Exception: "I believe" resurges to near-parity (~4:7 vs "I think") in technical 1:1 discussions with Trevor, used for precise technical claims ("I believe the OTEL library patches", "which I believe should be the easiest way forward").
